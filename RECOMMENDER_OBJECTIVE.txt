'The project aims to enhance user experience on the Agoda platform while providing analytical value to hotel owners. First, the system will be built based on Content-based Filtering, using cosine similarity combined with Gensim to measure similarity between hotels based on content features such as location, amenities, star rating, and descriptions. This approach allows users to receive recommendations tailored to their personal needs while minimizing search and comparison time.
Additionally, to improve accuracy and personalization, the system implements Collaborative Filtering using the ALS algorithm from the pyspark.ml.recommendation library. This model leverages customer behavior data—such as bookings, reviews, and ratings—to predict and provide recommendations that closely match actual preferences, evaluated via the RMSE metric to ensure reliability and effectiveness.
Beyond the customer focus, the system aims to provide in-depth insights for hotel owners through overview reports (name, star rating, address, average score); strength and weakness analysis based on detailed rating criteria and review content; customer behavior statistics by nationality, traveler group, and booking trends over time; and positive/negative keyword analysis to understand real-world experiences. These results are compared against system-wide averages and benchmarked against other hotels, helping owners adjust pricing, improve services, and build effective marketing strategies. Consequently, the system not only delivers a smart, personalized booking experience for users but also creates a powerful analytical tool to help partners boost competitiveness and business efficiency.'
