Agoda is a prominent online travel agency based in Singapore, founded in 2005 and currently owned by Booking Holdings Inc. As one of the world's leading booking platforms, Agoda offers a wide range of accommodations including hotels, apartments, villas, and other lodging types globally. The website allows users to search, compare, and book accommodations at competitive rates, enhancing the travel experience for millions of customers.<br><br>
However, Agoda currently lacks a comprehensive <b>Recommender System</b>—a crucial tool for personalizing the user experience and optimizing sales. The absence of an intelligent recommendation engine makes it difficult for users to find the hotels or resorts that best match their specific needs and preferences, especially given the overwhelming number of available options. This not only negatively impacts the customer experience but also reduces conversion rates and potential revenue for Agoda.<br><br>
Additionally, <b>hotel owners operating on Agoda</b> face another challenge: a lack of deep customer insights. Without a clear understanding of customer behavior, needs, and characteristics, partners struggle to devise optimal strategies regarding pricing, services, or promotions. This hinders their competitiveness and limits guest satisfaction.<br><br>
To address these issues, it is necessary to deploy a modern Recommender System that suggests the most suitable accommodation options based on browsing behavior, booking history, location, budget, and personal preferences. Simultaneously, a deep data analytics system should be developed for hotel owners to provide valuable insights, such as: customer demographics, peak booking times, review trends, cancellation behaviors, and key factors influencing booking decisions. This enables hotel owners to improve services, set reasonable prices, and optimize their business operations on the Agoda platform.
