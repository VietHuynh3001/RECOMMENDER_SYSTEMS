In the data mining process, analyzing key features helps to better understand customer behavior and needs. First, **rating scores** reflect overall service quality and customer satisfaction for each hotel, helping to identify factors that directly impact the stay experience. Additionally, analyzing **length of stay** helps identify short-term versus long-term booking trends, allowing hotels to optimize pricing policies and service packages. The **nationality** factor allows for customer segmentation by geography, which is useful for building marketing strategies and personalizing experiences. Analyzing **travel type** (solo, couple, family, business, etc.) also provides important insights for hotels to tailor their services to specific groups. Furthermore, data on **booked room types** and **detailed sub-ratings** helps visually identify a hotel's strengths and weaknesses. Finally, extracting **keywords from reviews** allows for a deeper analysis of customer sentiment and expectations. Classifying these into positive and negative keywords provides valuable insights for both the recommender system and hotel owners to improve service quality.
