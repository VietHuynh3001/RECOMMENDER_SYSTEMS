After building the recommendation models, evaluating the Recommender System is a crucial step to determine its effectiveness and practical application. This process goes beyond simply comparing the accuracy of algorithms like **Content-based Filtering** and **Collaborative Filtering**; it also considers personalization, recommendation coverage, and user satisfaction. Quantitative metrics like **RMSE**, combined with qualitative analysis of the user experience, will provide a comprehensive view to help select and optimize the best model for the Agoda system.
